Sporklet

Family meal plans, baby first.

Plan a week of meals for a baby and the grown-ups in one go. Rules you set, a shopping list that builds itself, and stored portions that count. No subscription.

In development · Coming to the App Store
What it does Privacy policy

Sporklet plans the week's meals for a family with a baby or a toddler, on your iPhone and iPad, with nothing to sign up for.

You set the rules once. How many meals a week with fish, egg, dairy, legumes or nuts, for the household and for each child. Which slots are difficult, so they get an easy meal. Which foods a child is too young for, from their date of birth. Which allergens are still to be introduced, and how many new ones a week. Sporklet then fills the week from your own recipes, keeps to the rules, and shows you why each meal was chosen.

What it does

One plan for everyone

Each recipe is marked baby-only or whole-family, and one week covers the baby and the adults.

Rules, not guesses

Minimum and maximum portions per food group per week, easy meals on difficult days, foods to avoid before an age, and a limit on new allergen groups per week.

Your recipes

Type them, paste them, import them from a link, or photograph a cookbook page. Ingredient lines with alternatives ("chicken or salmon") are understood.

Shopping list

A shopping list that builds itself from the week, split into one or two shops, with the things you always have kept out of it.

Stored portions

Mark a batch meal done and say what went into the fridge or the freezer; the planner serves those portions first and the list skips them.

Notes and favourites

Notes for next time, favourites, and a variety score that stops the same meal coming round too soon.

Share with your partner

Share the whole plan with your partner through iCloud. Both of you edit, both see the same week within seconds.

Widget and Undo

A home-screen widget with today's meals, and Undo for every change.

Free to download with a 14-day trial, then a one-off purchase. No subscription.

On the iPhone
And on the iPad
Private by design

Planning runs on your device with Apple's on-device models when available, and with Sporklet's own rules otherwise. No account, no server, no tracking. Your data lives in your iCloud.

Read the privacy policy
Made for one family first

Sporklet was built for households with one or multiple children. It comes with 48 real, published recipes to start from, and it expects you to add your own.

Sporklet does not give medical or nutritional advice. Allergen introduction and foods to avoid follow the app's tables, which you can edit; talk to your health visitor or doctor about your child.
